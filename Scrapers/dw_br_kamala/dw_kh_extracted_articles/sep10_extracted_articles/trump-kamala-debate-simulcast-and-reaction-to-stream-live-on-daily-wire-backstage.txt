Headline:
 Trump-Harris Debate Simulcast And Analysis Streaming Live On DailyWire+ 

Article Text:
 Watch live as Trump and Harris face off in debate, with expert analysis on DailyWire+.
The all-important debate between former President Donald Trump and Vice President Kamala Harris will stream live on DailyWire+ with pre-debate analysis on “Election Wire” and post-debate analysis on “Backstage.”
Before Trump and Harris take the stage, “Election Wire” will provide expert analysis on the ground in Philadelphia beginning at 7:30 p.m. ET. Daily Wire hosts Ben Shapiro, Matt Walsh, Michael Knowles, and Andrew Klavan, along with CEO Jeremy Boreing, will also discuss their pre-debate predictions and opinions on “Backstage” starting at 8:30 before the debate begins at 9:00.
Robert F. Kennedy Jr., the former presidential candidate who now backs Trump, will also join “Election Wire” to begin The Daily Wire’s debate coverage.

Viewers don’t need to tune in elsewhere for the debate itself as a live simulcast will be streamed on DailyWire+. Following the debate, the Daily Wire hosts will be back to give their reactions to the presidential fight night.
The Trump-Harris debate could be the only time the two presidential candidates face off before the election on November 5. The debate is being hosted by ABC News and moderated by David Muir and Linsey Davis.
To hear from the most trusted voices in conservative media on Tuesday night, join Ben Shapiro, Matt Walsh, Michael Knowles, Andrew Klavan, and Jeremy Boreing for full coverage and analysis and watch live on DailyWire+.
