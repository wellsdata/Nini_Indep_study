No dude has ever said this to another dude. No dude ever, when it’s not scripted by a woman.
No man has ever said, “Hey, dudes, you know, a bunch of ladies out there say we’re the problem. And you know what? They’re right. Some of us are totally the problem.” That’s not even the proper way to construct an ad.
You anticipate the ad continuing with: “They keep saying we’re the problem, but we’re not the problem. We’re the makers; we’re the people who are doing the dangerous jobs — and those are the people the Democrats support.”
That’s where you would think it’s going. But that’s not where it goes because they do not know how to speak to dudes. Why? Because it wasn’t a dude writing this ad. Likely, some woke 22-year-old female intern at an ad agency for White Dudes for Harris wrote this ad.
You thought they were going to appeal to men?
Wrong.
They’re basically saying that if you’re a man, the only way they can appeal to you is by desperately hoping you ignore Harris, whose picture appears one time in this ad.
The ad was paid for by Beige Rainbow PAC, where all the men go: to Beige Rainbow.
These people have no understanding of men.

She’s going to lose men by a thousand points. There are men not yet born who will vote against Harris.
Meanwhile, Harris herself is out on the campaign trail head-spinning, Linda-Blair-Exorcist style, word vomiting everywhere.
The narcissism on the Democratic side of the aisle is insane. Prime example: Harris, who was put in place by an upper-level coterie of the Democrats without ever having won a single primary vote and stands as the emptiest candidate in American history.
You can’t call her an empty vessel because there is no vessel. She is just a vacuum.

We love our country. I love our country. I know we all do. That’s why everybody’s here right now. We love our country. We take pride in the privilege of being American. And this is a moment where we can and must come together as Americans, understanding we have so much more in common than what separates us. Americans, by character, are people who have dreams and ambitions and aspirations. We believe in what is possible. We believe in what can be, and we believe in fighting for that. And that’s who we are. We believe in all that. And so this is a moment where we stand knowing what we are fighting for. We’re not fighting against. It’s what we’re fighting for.
To whom that garbage appeals deserves precisely what they receive. I don’t know how it’s possible to listen to that speech and not consider it the most vacuous bulls*** ever spoken. Furthermore, it went on for two minutes. I made it about 30 seconds before I started contemplating my life.
Her extraordinary narcissism and emptiness is characteristic of many politicians, an empty class of people who don’t actually have ideas. They have impulses and a narcissistic desire for glory.
